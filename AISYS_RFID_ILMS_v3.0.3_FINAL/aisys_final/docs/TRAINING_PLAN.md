# Training Plan

### 30-minute staff session
1. Sign-in and roles — 5 min.
2. Search and catalogue — 5 min.
3. RFID tagging and handheld inventory — 7 min.
4. Checkout/renew/checkin — 5 min.
5. Gate alerts and reports — 5 min.
6. Questions and troubleshooting — 3 min.

### Administrator session
Backups, restore, offline update, rollback, configuration, audit review and incident runbook.
